The Science of Napping

Napping can be a powerful tool for boosting energy, improving cognition, and enhancing mood. The key is timing and duration.

Nap Duration Guide

  • 10-20 minutes: Power nap. Improves alertness without sleep inertia.
  • 30 minutes: May cause sleep inertia (grogginess). Benefits include improved memory and creativity.
  • 60 minutes: Includes deep sleep. Good for memory consolidation but may cause grogginess.
  • 90 minutes: Full sleep cycle. Waking naturally, you'll feel most refreshed.

Best Time to Nap

The best time to nap is typically in the early afternoon (1-3 PM) when your body naturally experiences a dip in alertness.

Avoid napping too close to bedtime (within 6 hours) to prevent nighttime sleep disruption.
