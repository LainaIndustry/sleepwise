How to Use the Bedtime Calculator

Enter the time you want to wake up, select your preferred number of sleep cycles, and the calculator will suggest the best times to go to bed.

Understanding Sleep Cycles

A sleep cycle lasts about 90 minutes on average. During a complete sleep cycle, you go through light sleep, deep sleep, and REM sleep. Waking up at the end of a sleep cycle helps you feel more refreshed.

Why Bedtime Matters

Going to bed at the right time helps you get enough restorative sleep. It also helps regulate your circadian rhythm, making it easier to fall asleep and wake up naturally.
