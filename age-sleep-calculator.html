Why Sleep Needs Change with Age

Sleep requirements change throughout the lifespan due to developmental, physical, and environmental factors. Children need more sleep for growth and development, while adults typically need less but still require quality rest.

Key Factors Affecting Sleep Needs

  • Growth and Development: Children need more sleep for physical and cognitive development.
  • Hormonal Changes: Adolescence brings changes in circadian rhythm.
  • Lifestyle Factors: Stress, work, and daily activities affect sleep needs.
  • Health Conditions: Various medical conditions can affect sleep quality.
