Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how SleepWise ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to the website owners.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as:

  • Remembering your cookie consent preference
  • Remembering your theme preference (light/dark mode)
  • Ensuring the website loads correctly

These cookies cannot be disabled through our cookie consent banner.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. We use:

  • Google Analytics: To analyze website traffic and user behavior

These cookies collect information such as the number of visitors, pages visited, and the source of traffic.

Advertising Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver advertisements that are relevant to you and your interests. They may be used to:

  • Limit the number of times you see an advertisement
  • Measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns
  • Show advertisements based on your browsing habits

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. These third parties include:

  • Google: For analytics and advertising services

These third parties may use cookies to collect information about your online activities over time and across different websites.

Cookie Management

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences in several ways:

Our Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie consent banner that allows you to:

  • Accept all cookies - We will use all cookies as described
  • Reject non-essential cookies - We will only use essential cookies

Your preference will be saved in your browser's local storage.

Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to manage your cookie preferences through their settings. You can typically:

  • View and delete cookies
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies
  • Receive notifications when cookies are set

Here are links to cookie management instructions for popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google by visiting the Google Ad Settings page.

You can also opt out of many third-party advertising networks by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.
