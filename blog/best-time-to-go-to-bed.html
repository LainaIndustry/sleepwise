What Is the Best Time to Go to Bed?

📅 January 18, 2026 ✍️ SleepWise Editorial Team 📂 Sleep Schedule ⏱️ 7 min read

Finding the best time to go to bed can transform your sleep quality and overall health. But there's no single "perfect" bedtime that works for everyone. Your optimal bedtime depends on your wake-up time, sleep needs, and circadian rhythm.

This guide will help you discover your ideal bedtime and create a sleep schedule that leaves you feeling refreshed and energized.

Why Bedtime Matters

Going to bed at the right time isn't just about getting enough hours of sleep—it's about aligning with your body's natural rhythms. A consistent bedtime that matches your circadian rhythm can:

  • Improve sleep quality and depth
  • Help you fall asleep faster
  • Reduce nighttime awakenings
  • Support hormonal balance
  • Enhance daytime energy and focus
  • Strengthen your immune system

Understanding Your Circadian Rhythm

Your circadian rhythm is your body's internal 24-hour clock that regulates sleep-wake cycles, hormone release, and other bodily functions. It's influenced by light exposure and follows a natural pattern:

  • Early morning (6-8 AM): Cortisol rises, promoting wakefulness
  • Midday (12-2 PM): Small dip in alertness
  • Evening (8-10 PM): Melatonin rises, promoting sleepiness
  • Late night (2-4 AM): Deepest sleep occurs

Your ideal bedtime should fall within the window when your body naturally produces melatonin. For most people, this is between 9 PM and 11 PM.

How to Calculate Your Ideal Bedtime

Your ideal bedtime depends on two factors: your desired wake-up time and the number of sleep cycles you need. Most adults need 5-6 sleep cycles (7.5-9 hours) for optimal rest.

Here's a simple formula:

  1. Choose your wake-up time
  2. Subtract 15 minutes (time to fall asleep)
  3. Subtract 90 minutes × number of sleep cycles

Example: If you want to wake up at 7:00 AM and need 5 sleep cycles:

  • 7:00 AM - 15 min = 6:45 AM
  • 6:45 AM - (90 × 5) = 11:15 PM
  • Your bedtime should be around 11:15 PM

Recommended Bedtimes by Age

Different age groups have different sleep needs and natural sleep patterns. Here are general guidelines for recommended bedtimes:

Age Group Recommended Bedtime Wake-Up Time
Children (6-12) 8:00-9:00 PM 6:30-7:30 AM
Teenagers (13-17) 9:30-10:30 PM 6:30-8:00 AM
Adults (18-64) 10:00-11:30 PM 6:00-8:00 AM
Older Adults (65+) 9:00-10:30 PM 6:00-8:00 AM

Tips for a Consistent Bedtime

Finding your ideal bedtime is the first step. Maintaining it consistently is just as important. Here's how to stick to your schedule:

1. Start Gradually

If your current bedtime is far from your target, adjust gradually—15-30 minutes earlier each night until you reach your goal.

2. Create a Bedtime Routine

Develop relaxing rituals before bed to signal to your body that it's time to sleep. This could include reading, gentle stretching, or listening to calm music.

3. Be Consistent on Weekends

Try to maintain your bedtime on weekends as well. Large shifts in sleep timing can disrupt your circadian rhythm and make weekdays harder.

4. Avoid Sleep Disruptors

Avoid caffeine after 2 PM, limit alcohol consumption, and reduce screen time before bed.

5. Get Morning Sunlight

Expose yourself to bright light in the morning to reinforce your body's natural sleep-wake cycle.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best time to go to bed for 7 hours of sleep?

If you need to wake up at 7:00 AM, aim to be asleep by 11:45 PM (allowing 15 minutes to fall asleep). This gives you about 7 hours of sleep.

Is it better to go to bed early and wake up early?

Both morning and evening types can be healthy, as long as they get enough quality sleep. The best schedule is one that aligns with your natural circadian rhythm and daily obligations.

Can I change my natural bedtime?

Yes, you can shift your bedtime through consistent effort. It takes about 2-3 weeks of consistent behavior to shift your circadian rhythm by an hour.
