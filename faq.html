Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about sleep, calculators, and sleep health.

What is a sleep calculator?

A sleep calculator is a tool that helps you find your ideal bedtime or wake-up time based on sleep cycles, age, and personal preferences. It uses the average sleep cycle duration of about 90 minutes to suggest times that align with natural sleep patterns.

How does the sleep calculator work?

The sleep calculator works by taking your desired wake-up time or bedtime, the number of sleep cycles you want (typically 5-6 cycles), and the time it takes you to fall asleep. It then calculates times that allow you to complete full sleep cycles, helping you wake up at the end of a cycle for greater refreshment.

How many hours of sleep do adults need?

Most adults need 7-9 hours of sleep per night for optimal health and functioning. Individual needs may vary based on genetics, lifestyle, and overall health. The National Sleep Foundation recommends 7-9 hours for adults aged 26-64 years.

What is a sleep cycle?

A sleep cycle is a pattern of brain activity that repeats throughout the night, typically lasting about 90 minutes. Each cycle includes stages of light sleep, deep sleep, and REM (dream) sleep. Most adults go through 4-6 sleep cycles per night.

How long is a sleep cycle?

A sleep cycle typically lasts about 90 minutes. However, sleep cycles can vary between 70-120 minutes depending on individual factors. The first cycle of the night is often shorter, while later cycles may be longer.

What time should I go to bed?

The best time to go to bed depends on when you want to wake up and how many sleep cycles you need. Most adults feel best with 7-9 hours of sleep, which equals about 5-6 sleep cycles. Use our bedtime calculator to find your personalized bedtime.

Read more about the best time to go to bed →

What time should I wake up?

The ideal wake-up time aligns with the end of a sleep cycle, making you feel more refreshed. Use our wake-up calculator to find the best time based on your bedtime and desired sleep cycles.

Does the sleep calculator guarantee better sleep?

No, the sleep calculator provides estimates and recommendations based on sleep science, but it cannot guarantee better sleep. Sleep quality depends on many factors including sleep environment, stress, health conditions, and lifestyle habits.

Why do I still feel tired after sleeping?

Feeling tired after sleeping can have many causes: waking mid-cycle, poor sleep quality, sleep disorders like sleep apnea, stress, diet, or underlying health conditions. If you consistently feel tired, consider improving sleep hygiene and consulting a healthcare professional.

Can naps affect nighttime sleep?

Yes, naps can affect nighttime sleep. Napping late in the day or for too long can disrupt your natural sleep-wake cycle and make it harder to fall asleep at night. For most people, brief naps (10-20 minutes) in the early afternoon are best.

How can I improve my sleep schedule?

To improve your sleep schedule, maintain consistent bedtimes and wake times even on weekends, expose yourself to natural light during the day, avoid screens before bed, create a relaxing bedtime routine, and limit caffeine and alcohol close to bedtime.
