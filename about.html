About SleepWise

Helping you plan better sleep and wake up refreshed.

Our Mission

SleepWise is dedicated to helping people understand and improve their sleep through free, accessible, and accurate sleep calculators and educational resources.

We believe that better sleep leads to better health, productivity, and quality of life. Our goal is to make evidence-based sleep information available to everyone.

What We Provide

SleepWise offers a comprehensive suite of sleep calculators and tools:

  • Sleep Calculator: Find your ideal bedtime or wake-up time
  • Bedtime Calculator: Determine the best time to go to bed
  • Wake-Up Calculator: Find the optimal time to wake up
  • Sleep Cycle Calculator: Analyze your sleep cycles
  • Sleep Debt Calculator: Track your weekly sleep patterns
  • Sleep Duration Calculator: Calculate your total sleep time
  • Nap Calculator: Plan the perfect nap
  • Age-Based Sleep Guide: Sleep recommendations by age

How Our Calculators Work

Our calculators are based on established sleep science research. The average sleep cycle is approximately 90 minutes, and we use this as the foundation for our calculations.

Each calculator provides estimates based on user inputs such as bedtime, wake-up time, age, and desired sleep cycles. We include clear disclaimers to ensure users understand these are estimates, not medical advice.

Editorial Approach

Our blog articles and educational content are created with a commitment to accuracy, clarity, and usefulness. We draw on reputable sources including:

  • Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)
  • National Institutes of Health (NIH)
  • National Sleep Foundation
  • American Academy of Sleep Medicine
  • Peer-reviewed sleep research

Content Standards

We adhere to strict content standards:

  • All content is thoroughly researched and fact-checked
  • We clearly distinguish between established scientific findings and emerging research
  • We include appropriate disclaimers for health-related information
  • We cite sources where appropriate
  • We avoid sensationalism and unsubstantiated claims

Commitment to Education

SleepWise is committed to providing useful, educational information that empowers users to make informed decisions about their sleep. We are not a medical provider, and our content should never replace professional medical advice.
